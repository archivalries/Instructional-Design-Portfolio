Recursos para aprender español

Free and course-linked resources for practicing Spanish outside class. Open a section to explore.

Clave
  • Origen
  • Género
  • Nivel
  • Costo
  • Tipo

Knowt

  • Free
  • Flashcards

A free alternative to Quizlet, which has put most of its features behind a paywall. Students join our Knowt class to find the course sets, or start with the 500 most common Spanish words.

500 Most Common Spanish Words Class link shared with enrolled students

Duolingo

  • Free
  • App

Short, game-like lessons that help you build a daily habit. It is no replacement for class, but if it gets you studying, who am I to say no?

Sharpen

  • Included with your course materials
  • App

McGraw Hill's study app, built around our textbook and the assignments you complete in Connect. You already have access through your course materials, so use it to review and take practice quizzes.

Language Reactor

  • Free
  • Chrome extension

A free Chrome extension that shows Spanish and English subtitles side by side on Netflix and YouTube. Click any word for a translation, or replay a line as many times as you need.

A playlist I put together of Spanish-language songs from across genres and countries. Pick one song a week, look up the lyrics, and listen for words you already know.

Tip: Install Language Reactor (see Apps/Extensions) to watch with Spanish and English subtitles side by side.

Alguien tiene que morir

  • España
  • Suspenso

A three-episode thriller set in 1950s Spain, where a son's return from Mexico unsettles his family. Short enough to finish in a weekend.

Un cuento perfecto

  • España
  • Romance

A romantic comedy about two people from very different worlds, full of quick, everyday dialogue.

El vecino

  • España
  • Comedia

An ordinary guy in Madrid gets superpowers and has no idea what to do with them. Funny, casual, and packed with colloquial Spanish from Spain.

La casa de papel

  • España
  • Acción

The heist thriller that became a worldwide hit. It moves fast, so turn on Spanish subtitles to keep up.

La casa de las flores

  • México
  • Comedia

A dark comedy about a wealthy family and their flower shop, and a good way to hear Mexican Spanish and its humor.

Las chicas del cable

  • España
  • Drama

A period drama about four young women working as telephone operators in 1920s Madrid.

Erre que ELE

  • España
  • Intermedio y avanzado

Lucía, a Spanish philologist and teacher, teaches the real, colloquial Spanish people speak in Spain. It is aimed at intermediate and advanced learners, so save it for when you want a challenge.

ProfeDeELE

  • A1–B1

Video lessons organized into A1, A2, and B1 playlists. Start with A1 and A2, which line up with SPAN 1410 and 1420.
