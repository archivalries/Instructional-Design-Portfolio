H5P · Beginning Spanish I

H5P Samples

One example of each H5P activity type I use in SPAN 1410. Each targets a single grammar or vocabulary point, gives immediate feedback, and allows unlimited retries with the option to view solutions. Try them below.

Dictation

Lección 13 | Dictado | Estar

Learners hear short sentences with estar and type what they hear, then check their spelling and accents.

Drag the Words

Lección 16 | Arrastra la palabra | Ser

Learners drag the correct form of ser into a short narrative about a family.

Fill in the Blanks

Lección 17 | Rellenar el espacio | Ser / Estar

A story-based passage that asks learners to choose between ser and estar in context.

Crossword

Lección 18 | Crucigrama | Familiares

Family vocabulary practiced through relationship clues, including a bonus word for step-family.
