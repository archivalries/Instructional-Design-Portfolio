RIFF

Interactive Examples

RIFF is a conversational assistant embedded in the SPAN 2310 correspondence course. Each activity is scoped to a module objective, and RIFF is set up to coach rather than to write for learners.

Course assistant

Meet RIFF

RIFF is introduced at the start of the course with a welcome message, so learners know what it can and cannot do for them before they meet it in an activity.

Module activity · Present tense

RIFF Writing Partner: Present Tense

RIFF greets learners in Spanish and supports them as they write about themselves and their families in the present tense.

Module activity · Past tenses

RIFF Writing Coach: Past Tenses

Learners paste a draft written in the past tenses (preterite and imperfect) and say how far along they are; RIFF points out what is worth a second look and asks questions that help them revise, without rewriting anything for them.
