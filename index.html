Instructional Design Portfolio

Wyatt Ince, M.A.

I design and teach Canvas courses across the first- and second-year Spanish sequence at Texas State University, building accessible, interactive materials grounded in how adults learn.

About

I am an Assistant Professor of Instruction in the Department of World Languages & Literatures at Texas State University, where I have designed and taught Canvas courses across the first- and second-year Spanish sequence since 2021. Before and alongside that work, I led curriculum design as Lead Instructor at Freestyle Languages and managed a remote team of editors and subject matter experts as Managing Editor at InklyAi.

My M.A. focused on Second Language Acquisition and Teaching: how adults acquire complex skills, how to sequence content, and how to measure growth. That question shapes every course I build.

Work samples

The work samples are grouped into five sets, each showing one set of skills. Several come from my SPAN 2310 correspondence course, which I developed in Canvas with TXST Online through the ADDIE process and which was reviewed against the Quality Matters (QM) rubric; others come from my face-to-face first-year Spanish courses.

DesignPlus · Canvas

DesignPlus Samples

Two content pages built with DesignPlus. A present perfect grammar lesson, framed around Stranger Things, uses color coding with a key, tooltips, alternating content blocks, and self-checking Quick Checks. A resource guide for learning Spanish organizes apps, music, shows, and YouTube channels into an accordion and tabs, each with a short annotation.

  • Visual hierarchy

H5P

H5P Samples

One example of each H5P activity type I use in Beginning Spanish I: a dictation, a drag-the-words task, a fill-in-the-blanks passage, and a crossword on family vocabulary. Each targets a single grammar or vocabulary point, gives immediate feedback, and allows unlimited retries, so learners can practice to mastery before assessment.

  • Immediate feedback
  • Mastery practice

YuJa · H5P

Video Examples

Interactive video built with YuJa and H5P: video lessons from the correspondence course with embedded questions that pause the video for practice and subtitles throughout, plus an on-screen overlay added in YuJa that corrects an error in the recorded content without re-recording. The module also includes a YuJa video quiz on commands (mandatos) whose questions appear at set points as learners watch.

  • Captions
  • Embedded questions
  • Overlays

RIFF

Interactive Examples

RIFF, an embedded conversational assistant introduced at the start of the course, and two RIFF activities aligned to module objectives: a writing partner that supports learners as they describe themselves and their families in the present tense, and a writing coach that helps them revise drafts in the past tenses by asking questions rather than rewriting for them.
